# Questions for the owner

The drawing uses the coordinator's recommended answers below. These are choices for the drawing review. Approve the drawing before opening build slices.

| Question | Drawn with | Alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. What should the first use do? | Make the workflow every client gets available on the deal, with the common to-dos for its kind, applicable additions, sourced rules of thumb, market observations, and lender program terms. | Start with a standalone, read-only practice guide and attach it to the deal workflow later. |
| 2. What belongs in the move? | Business vertical guides, playbooks, procedures, rubrics, and conventions. Keep business lessons as references. Keep engineering and agent-operating instructions in their current homes. | Move only material needed for the first deal workflow. Keep the rest in the old archive until a partner asks for it. |
| 3. Who can decide and share? | Either partner can propose, approve, or retire shared knowledge. Private items belong to one partner. Record every approval with who and when. A direct partner explicitly approves the exact client release. | Require the other partner to approve shared changes or designate one partner to approve client releases. |
| 4. How do exceptions work? | Practice type and practice stage drive applicability. Market narrows guidance. A client or deal can add a to-do or override a value with a reason. Common to-dos stay visible. A skip needs a reason. Deal exceptions take precedence over client exceptions. | Use deal exceptions only, or name specific common to-dos that cannot be skipped. |
| 5. What instructions does Doc get from the database? | Read current workflow to-dos, rules of thumb, market observations and program terms through the engine. Keep tool rules, access checks and confirmed saves in the checked-in Doc instructions and engine doors. | Give Doc sourced material only in its first use. Keep its current workflow order until the app cutover proves parity. |
| 6. What does the client see? | The proposed copy shows the applicable to-dos and reviewed shared guidance. It leaves progress and dates internal until the owner selects what to include. Download an exact approved copy through the deal. | Include agreed progress and milestones. Decide separately whether released copies should appear on the client website. |
| 7. Which practice traits and starting content should ship? | Use reviewed keys for recorded practice types and stages. Start with the current next-database checklist variants, an unconditional fallback, and the seven measures in the [table drawing](tables.md#practice-traits-and-knowledge). Reconcile each deal kind and timing key before cutover. | Approve a wider vocabulary or new workflow variants before import. Supply the ordered common to-dos for any new variant. |

The build needs the owner's choice of starting to-dos where the current code has no workflow, the first practice type and stage labels, and the first source material to approve. List those private source keys in the offline import review. Record approved design choices in [Decisions](../decisions.md).
