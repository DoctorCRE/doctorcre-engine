# Search

- [ ] Before a search, the page shows the search box and its Search button. Typing searches after a short pause. Search or Enter submits immediately. Clearing the box clears the results.
- [ ] Search finds people, companies and deals by name. A full email address or domain finds the people and companies with that stored address. A bare domain also finds records with that exact name.
- [ ] Each person or company appears once, with its roles, city when recorded and the names of linked deals. People or companies with the same name each get their own result.
- [ ] Each result's name opens its record. Deals open their deal page. Clients and vendors open their directory record. Leads on the Leads board open their drawer. Other people and companies open their record in Relationships. Each linked deal name opens its deal page.
- [ ] The result count covers the visible records. Deal phases and owners use the words shown on Deals. The screen shows no registry references, record IDs or internal labels.
- [ ] After a search, Scope narrows the visible results. Saved views can save, open, rename or reset searches on this device.
