# Leads

- **For** both partners, signed in, on a computer or phone. Each can see leads assigned to either partner and leads waiting to be claimed.
- **Must** show each eligible lead once on the board, in its stage. Open its name to see the lead's details.

## Board

- [ ] The header counts leads matching all current filters, including cards beyond the first five in a stage. The first load says “Loading leads…” until the count is known. An empty filter result offers Reset filters.
- [ ] Search, Owner, Market, Specialty and Stage narrow the same board. Owner includes Unclaimed. Stage includes Opportunity and Archived. Contact evidence appears when there are both reachable and research leads.
- [ ] Quiet leads filters the board to leads the follow-up read says are due. It combines with the other filters and uses the same count.
- [ ] Stage names and counts sit above a thin divider. Spacing separates the columns. On a computer, stages share one row that can scroll sideways. A selected stage uses the board's width. Columns stack on a phone, with controls at least 44 pixels tall and wide.
- [ ] Each card shows the lead's name and owner, or “Unclaimed.” An overdue quiet follow-up adds one “Follow-up late” badge. A newly created email lead shows “New from email” until it is acknowledged. Change stage by using the drawer or moving the card and confirming the change.
- [ ] Claim appears on every unclaimed lead outside Archived and gives it to the partner who clicks. Claimed leads and archived leads have no Claim action.
- [ ] Territory shows market counts and an optional map. Picking a market filters the board. A marker can open its lead or a chooser for that place. The board is the list of leads across markets.
- [ ] Reasons to reach out shows recorded reasons and their sources. Source coverage appears for sources with coverage or blocked updates.

## Drawer

- [ ] The lead's name opens its drawer with stage, contact details, plans, timing, research, correspondence and stage history. Older correspondence and stage changes load on request.
- [ ] A quiet lead's drawer shows its last contact date or when it was added if no contact was recorded. It also shows when follow-up is due.
- [ ] Claim follows the board's rule. Link appears for suggested clients and uses the selected match. Once linked, the lead leaves the board.
- [ ] If a Claim or Link save is unconfirmed, Check outcome is available in the drawer and checks the same request.
- [ ] Stage changes open a review before saving. Archiving requires a reason. An automatic move offers Undo when it can be reversed.

## Leads from email

- [ ] A clear inbound lead creates one lead at New, with no owner until a partner claims it. Both partners can find the unclaimed lead on Leads.
- [ ] A recognized lead-source notice or a direct inquiry qualifies when it gives a nonblank prospect name, a clear request for help and an email address, phone number or street address with city. A lead-source notice takes the prospect's details from the body. The street address and city must both appear in the quoted evidence. An exact email or phone match can identify an existing lead, including a contact fact verified by a later notice.
- [ ] Internal mail, system notices and mail about an existing deal or relationship do not create new leads. An unknown address or a subject alone is insufficient. Keep uncertain intake with its recorded evidence for reclassification. Work that needs a separate partner decision goes through its own Home kind.
- [ ] The lead's correspondence includes the exact source email and its saved summary, when available. If the email has not been filed before, save it with the lead. Repeated intake of the same message links it once. More messages from the same verified prospect go to the same lead without changing its owner or resetting a cleared badge.
- [ ] The Leads row shows **New from email** until either partner opens the lead's drawer successfully or claims it. Clearing this badge records a shared acknowledgement and does not assign an owner. Claim assigns the partner and clears the badge in the same save. Scanning the list or previewing a row leaves the badge set.
- [ ] Creating this lead produces no Home card. A distinct action that still needs a partner can appear on Home under its own kind.
