# Leads

- **For** both partners, signed in, on a computer or phone. Each can see leads assigned to either partner and leads waiting to be claimed.
- **Must** show each eligible lead once on the board, in its stage. Open its name to see the lead's details.

## Board

- [ ] The count beside the Leads title counts leads matching all current filters, including cards beyond the first five in a stage. The title also shows when the board was last updated. The first load says “Loading leads…” until the count is known. An empty filter result offers Reset filters.
- [ ] The board starts reading leads while sign-in is checked, and shows nothing until sign-in is confirmed. A failed refresh shows a short warning above the board.
- [ ] Search, Owner, Market, Specialty, Quiet leads, Contact evidence and Stage share one toolbar row on a computer and narrow the same board. Owner includes Unclaimed. Stage includes Opportunity and Archived. Contact evidence appears when there are both reachable and research leads.
- [ ] Quiet leads filters the board to due follow-ups using the latest saved contact and current open next steps. It combines with the other filters and shows “Showing N quiet leads” using the same count.
- [ ] Stage names and counts sit above a thin divider. Spacing separates the columns. On a computer, the board fills the screen below the toolbar and the page does not scroll. Each stage scrolls inside itself. Stages with no leads leave the board while others have some. When every stage that has leads is at least about 204 pixels wide, they share the board's width and nothing scrolls sideways, so five stages fit beside an open Today at 1440 wide. Otherwise whole stages at least 280 pixels wide share the width, and the rest scroll sideways and stop at a stage's edge. The stage header row shows “N more ›” when stages sit off to the right and a back arrow, read aloud as “N earlier stages”, when they sit off to the left. A selected stage uses the board's width. Columns stack on a phone, with controls at least 44 pixels tall and wide.
- [ ] Each card shows the lead's name and owner, or “Unclaimed.” An overdue quiet follow-up adds one “Follow-up late” badge. A newly created email lead shows “New from email” until it is acknowledged. A name stored in all capitals reads in title case. Credentials and acronyms such as LLC, DDS and US keep their capitals. Change stage by using the drawer or moving the card and confirming the change.
- [ ] Claim appears on every unclaimed lead outside Archived and gives it to the partner who clicks. Claimed leads and archived leads have no Claim action.
- [ ] Territory is one line under Reasons to reach out. It shows market counts and an optional map. On a computer, markets that do not fit on one line fold into a +N chip that shows the rest, and **Fewer** folds them again. A market name too long for the line truncates and shows its full name in the hover tip. Picking a market filters the board. The map opens on all 19 of the partners' markets, Mobile to Tallahassee and down the coast, on a computer and a phone. Each marker shows its lead count, from the same counts as the market chips; a lead with a city but no state is placed when that city name is found once. Markers that would touch merge, and clicking one zooms in. A marker for one place shows its name where there is room. A marker can open its lead or a chooser for that place. The board is the list of leads across markets.
- [ ] Reasons to reach out is a one-line strip that opens. It shows a count of the reasons it shows, such as “(4)”, with “+” when there are more. Open, it shows recorded reasons and their sources. Home's “All reasons” link opens it. Source coverage appears for sources with coverage or blocked updates.

## Drawer

- [ ] The lead's name opens its drawer with owner, stage, contact details, plans, timing, research, correspondence and stage history. Older correspondence and stage changes load on request.
- [ ] Plans and research use plain words. An old record code in the notes, such as `C-013`, reads as that record's name and opens it. Handling advice appears when it applies to this lead. Phones use a readable format, estimated lease dates are labeled, and dates outside this year include the year.
- [ ] A quiet lead's drawer shows its last contact date or says no contact was recorded and shows when it was added. It also shows when follow-up is due.
- [ ] Claim follows the board's rule. “Link to client” names the suggested client and uses the selected match. Once linked, the lead leaves the board.
- [ ] If a Claim or Link save is unconfirmed, Check outcome is available in the drawer and checks the same request.
- [ ] Stage changes open a review before saving. Archiving requires a reason. The drawer says when the lead was archived because it became a deal. An automatic move offers Undo when it can be reversed.

## Leads from email

- [ ] Intake recognizes a lead-source notice by matching its sender's address or domain against the [lead-source sender list](../new-database.md#leads-from-email).
- [ ] A clear inbound lead creates one lead at New, with no owner until a partner claims it. Both partners can find the unclaimed lead on Leads.
- [ ] A recognized lead-source notice or a direct inquiry qualifies when it gives a nonblank prospect name, a clear request for help and an email address, phone number or street address with city. A known lead source's notice is itself the request for help, so it needs no request label. A lead-source notice takes the prospect's details from the body. The street address and city must both appear in the quoted evidence. An exact email or phone match can identify an existing lead, including a contact fact verified by a later notice.
- [ ] Internal mail, system notices and mail about an existing deal or relationship do not create new leads. An unknown address or a subject alone is insufficient. Keep uncertain intake with its recorded evidence for reclassification. Work that needs a separate partner decision goes through its own Home kind.
- [ ] The drawer identifies the source email even after the badge is acknowledged. The lead's correspondence includes the exact source email and its saved summary, when available. If the email has not been filed before, save it with the lead. Repeated intake of the same message links it once. More messages from the same verified prospect go to the same lead without changing its owner or resetting a cleared badge.
- [ ] The source email keeps the full captured body. When capture supplies the sender's text and quoted history separately, the engine keeps both alongside that body.
- [ ] The Leads row shows **New from email** until either signed-in partner acknowledges it after successfully opening the drawer or claims it. Acknowledgement clears the badge for both partners and leaves ownership as it was. Claim assigns the partner and clears the badge in the same save. Scanning the list, previewing a row, and loading the drawer leave the badge set until the app sends the acknowledgement.
- [ ] Creating this lead produces no Home row. A distinct action that still needs a partner can appear on Home under its own kind.
- [ ] A clear lead-source notice already on Home can move to Leads with its exact stored email. Replaying that notice keeps its existing lead, owner, and history.
