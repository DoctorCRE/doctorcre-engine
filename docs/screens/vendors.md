# Vendors

## Vendors list

Each vendor row shows the last contact date only.

- [ ] On a computer a header row under the toolbar names the columns: Name, Category, Territory, Location, Last contacted, Owner. It stays in place while the list scrolls. Screen readers skip it, and each row still reads its own labels.
- [ ] On a computer each vendor row is one line. A vendor with deal history (Referred, Worked, Win rate) takes two lines.
- [ ] An empty list shows one line, such as "No vendors match these filters", with Clear all filters when filters are on.
- [ ] On a phone a vendor's last contact and owner share one line.

On a computer the list starts on the first screen and the page does not scroll. Each vendor is one row, with each fact in its own column. Search, Category, Stage, Disposition, Sort, Territory, Reset, refresh and Show quiet vendors share one toolbar row that stays in place. Filters and search apply as they change, so a computer shows no Apply. On a phone, filters fold under Filters and sort with Apply and Reset.

Vendor rows share one column grid, so Category, Territory, Location and Last contacted line up row to row. Filters show their whole choice. Slash-joined territories wrap after a slash, never mid-word.

The vendor count and the All, Joe and Dell chips sit in the top bar beside the title. On a phone the count is not shown, so the owner chips fit. People worth meeting is a tab beside the list. Today starts collapsed on Vendors and remembers the partner's choice.

A deal count of zero is hidden in the row and the drawer, because the engine sends zero when no deal history is recorded. The drawer labels the owner.

- [ ] A vendor's Territory shows once in the drawer, under Where, and not again above the contact list.
- [ ] A person vendor is listed under Contacts & offices with their own phone, mobile and email. The drawer does not repeat Phone, Mobile or Email elsewhere. A contact shows Phone and Mobile as separate fields. A company vendor keeps its own company line.
- [ ] Contact status reads "Active", "Nurture", "Paused" or "Do not contact", never the stored code, in the vendor and client drawers.
- [ ] A drawer section with nothing to show is not drawn. A vendor with no deal history and no note has no Partnership block, and the last contact draws no second divider over the next section.

## Meeting planner

Research shows vendor contacts and their possible meetings. For a person with a vendor role, it shows that person. For a company with a vendor role, it shows the people with open company links, or a row with no contact. Each vendor link uses the party ID. Copied vendors keep their recorded vendor ref.

Search covers the vendor ref, contact and company names, category, service territory, and contact city. Contact state is outside the search because the new database does not store it. Pages follow the vendor and contact IDs. Start a new search after records change.

The planner drops the outside-market warning because no production vendors used the flag and the new database does not carry it over.

A possible meeting comes from the signed-in partner's stored Outlook agenda. Its organizer or an attendee must match the contact's email exactly after normalization. Research still shows contacts when the calendar is unavailable.

The new database has no verified office findings for vendor contacts. The planner serves only Research and refuses Suggested like any unknown view. Research keeps the existing missing-office label.
