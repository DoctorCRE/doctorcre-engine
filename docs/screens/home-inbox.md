# Home inbox

Home gives a signed-in partner the work that needs their attention and a place to finish it. Check each line on a computer and a phone against a practice copy. The examples use made-up names.

## The dashboard

- [ ] The first view starts with work tiles. Home has no right panel, hero, greeting, date banner, section explainers, Mail priorities, Refresh inbox, or Search inbox.
- [ ] The tiles sit side by side on a computer and form one column on a phone. Each tile shows its item count and previews its first few cards. **See all** opens the rest of that tile's list when there are more cards. Empty tiles stay off the screen.
- [ ] **Reminders** shows due-today and late deal and vendor follow-ups and decisions. It also shows undated deal and vendor follow-ups and pending work such as reply drafts, suggestion reviews, lease facts, and tour feedback that need a partner. **Today** and **Needs your attention** have no separate sections.
- [ ] **Late** appears within **Reminders** only when a deal or vendor follow-up or decision is past due. Show how late in Central calendar days, such as "3 days late". Future follow-ups and automatic check-ins first appear on their due day.
- [ ] A nightly checklist reminder first appears when the deal owner's [morning window](../new-database.md#deal-watches-and-nightly-checklist-reminders) opens.
- [ ] **In motion and waiting** shows work still being processed, held, or snoozed. Each card says what it is waiting for and when it returns, if known. A snoozed card offers **Wake**.
- [ ] Home reminders, Today, and Calendar include steps on deals, buildings, parties with a vendor role, and people at one or more current vendor companies. A vendor qualifies even when the party or one of the person's companies also has a lead role. A company link is current if its start is unknown or on or before today's Central date and its end is absent or after today. Lead-only steps open from the lead on [Leads](leads.md), with the follow-up attention badge when late.
- [ ] **Today's meetings** appears as one tile when the calendar has meetings for the current Central day. Identify each occurrence by its Outlook event key so separate invites with the same title and time each appear. A meeting stays in the tile through the end of the day, even after a partner answers its review. Open it to see its attendees, location, and deal link when available. If Outlook supplies an outcome review for a meeting in the tile, review it there. An outstanding review from an earlier day belongs in **Reminders**. A stale calendar shows the last successful sync time.

  Full details for ended meetings depend on the Outlook publisher sending their calendar rows. While it sends only a review for an ended meeting, the tile can show the title and times from that review. [Outlook publisher change](https://github.com/DoctorCRE/carr-system/issues/3265)
- [ ] Home has no **Done today** or **Earlier completions**. Finished work remains openable from the affected record's history with any Undo that is still available there.
- [ ] Home checks for changes on its existing one-minute schedule while visible and online. A scheduled refresh adds new cards and removes finished cards in place. Existing cards keep their identity and relative order. The refresh preserves scroll position, keyboard focus, the open review, and unsaved edits. If an open item finishes elsewhere, the review reports that it finished and returns focus to the next item or the tile heading when closed.
- [ ] If a read fails, the page reports which information is unavailable and keeps the last successful view when one exists. Meeting failure leaves the other tiles usable.

## Every card

- [ ] Show each step, Outlook conversation, recorded call, or recorded meeting once. Match by recorded source and target. Without a conversation ID, use the exact stored email. Two distinct steps with the same wording remain separate.
- [ ] A step uses its saved title when it has one. Otherwise use the whole instruction after display cleanup if it fits within 90 visible characters. Use the record name as the heading when the instruction does not fit or is blank. Show the full instruction below the heading when it differs from the heading. Keep phone numbers, link destinations, and meeting details in the instruction. A link with balanced parentheses keeps its entire destination. If a link is unclear, display the stored instruction verbatim.
- [ ] A pending decision uses the title for its kind and the recorded name, such as "Choose the deal for Maya's email". Put the saved summary, instruction, or feedback in the why line so a partner can decide what to do.
- [ ] Mail sent by either partner never asks either partner which deal or vendor it belongs to, whichever mailbox it is in. It is still queued for automatic filing. A partner's addresses are their stored contact addresses.
- [ ] A card shows its due date once. The why line does not repeat it.
- [ ] Click or press Enter on the card title to open the exact email, record, or review. Dates use Central time and read as words. Keep internal codes, schedule offsets, status prefixes, raw timestamps, and Markdown out of display text when it can be cleaned up reliably. Preserve the saved evidence.
- [ ] Put the action that finishes or opens the review in the same place on actionable cards. Offer **Snooze** and **Drop** where supported. Snooze sets a return time. Drop requires a reason. **Reject** with a reason finishes a lease fact review. Home has no Hide or set-waiting-person control.
- [ ] A successful finish removes the card immediately. Finishes elsewhere take effect on the next refresh. Show a temporary Undo after a save that supports it. Undo brings back the exact card. Once a reply is sent or a suggestion is decided, the action is final.
- [ ] When cards are selected, show the count, **Snooze selected**, and **Clear selection**. Show each batch result with the available Undo and retry controls. Retry uses the same batch when the original result is uncertain.

Home shows call evidence to both partners. When the other partner receives an assigned follow-up, its card opens the step. Assigned suggestions and completion reviews show their step wording and keep their decision controls.

Use the existing step saves in door 18, suggestion decisions in door 21, reply delivery in door 22, and Home marks in door 24. A pending draft leads its conversation card. Any pending suggestions stay reviewable on that card. When the draft stops being pending, the next decision leads the card with any delivery state still available. While delivery is uncertain, show it in **In motion and waiting** as the system checks Sent. A newer draft or a recorded Outlook reply retires a superseded draft.

## Email and reply review

- [ ] A link to a draft opens that draft even when another reply leads the conversation card. A suggestion link opens its Home card. Both show the pending decisions for the conversation. If the linked review has finished, show its recorded disposition and any saved reason. Links to another partner's work are unavailable.
- [ ] An older filing link opens the current card when a newer email carries the same pending decision. A linked card in **In motion and waiting** is read-only and shows what it is waiting for, such as "Snoozed until Nov 4, 2026, 10:00 AM Central". **Back to Home** returns to the dashboard, where a snoozed card offers **Wake**.
- [ ] The email card uses the saved action title and summary from the exact message behind the pending draft or leading decision. Show the number of decisions when more than one remains. While the summary is queued, or when the saved result has no grounded action, use the title for the pending decision and show the saved request, summary or recorded facts in the why line. The subject is available in the source view.
- [ ] Opening an email, draft, or email suggestion shows the source thread once. Each message from the signed-in partner's mailbox appears once as "From Maya · Nov 2", even when a draft and a suggestion point to different copies. Show the text that person wrote, with its paragraphs, inline answers, and postscripts. When there is earlier text, **Show earlier text** reveals reply headers, quoted history, signatures, footers, and banners for that message. A message with only earlier text still has its sender, date, and disclosure control. Older messages that could not be recovered from Outlook use the existing cleanup and keep some text when their saved body is nonempty. See [Authored text in Home email](../new-database.md#authored-text-in-home-email).
- [ ] The reply review shows one draft with **Edit** and **Talk to Doc about this draft**. The draft area names the person whose message it answers, such as "Your reply to Maya", and contains the draft, its To and CC, and the send controls. While the review is open, **Send reply** is the only primary button on the screen. An automatic draft starts with `Re: ` followed by the source email's subject. The partner can edit the draft subject before sending.
- [ ] When a reply's recipients were refused (`submission_recipients_invalid`), the card says "Didn't send. The recipients were refused. Correct them and send again." The review fills **To** and **CC** with the saved addresses. The partner can correct them and press **Send reply** again. Any other final failure says "Reply from Outlook".
- [ ] **Edit** opens the pending draft for manual changes. **Talk to Doc about this draft** opens a conversation with its exact draft and email. Doc proposes revised text. **Save revision** applies the confirmed text to that same pending draft at the version Doc reviewed. Return to the same review with the revised text. The partner still presses **Send reply** to send it. If the draft changed or is no longer pending, show that in the review and preserve any unsaved text. See [Doc](doc.md).

## Work the engine handles

- [ ] A clear inbound lead goes straight to Leads as an unclaimed lead with its source email and **New from email** badge. Repeated lead-source notices about the same verified prospect keep one lead with its distinct evidence. See [Leads](leads.md).
- [ ] When a stored lead-source notice moves to Leads, its pending filing decisions finish with **Moved to Leads** in their history. The conversation keeps its reply drafts, other decisions, and filings for other messages.
- [ ] The engine files mail when saved evidence identifies the destination, such as a known thread tied to one deal. When filing judgment is enabled, a job on the mailbox partner's link saves relevant facts on each verified deal or vendor. The engine checks the sender's vendor affiliation on the date the email was sent. Each saved fact keeps its source email. A partner's filing or dismissal closes the email's pending filing reviews in both mailboxes. A new correction from the mail router stays reviewable. A prior unlink stays in effect, and an independent reply or suggestion still has its review. When a job cannot resolve the filing, Home keeps the existing filing review or shows **Review where to file this email**. The review offers **Choose deal** and **Choose vendor** when those destinations apply. The partner can ask Doc for help with it.
- [ ] Choosing a deal checks that deal before enabling **File email**. If the email is already filed to another deal or the selected deal's client isn't linked to the email, show the reason the engine gives and require a short explanation. If the requirement changes before the save, keep the selected deal and prompt for the reason beside the field.
- [ ] Routine schedule bookkeeping, informational deal updates, and work with a recorded completion stay with their record. An unfinished checklist reminder or distinct proposed call note can still need a partner. A scheduled check-in can ask the partner to contact someone on the due day.

Today's engine reads steps, marks, suggestions, drafts, and meetings. It saves email summaries and can already file a link when routing evidence identifies one eligible deal. The dashboard, filtering, and clean thread use stored records and require no model. The review reads use the same display entry for each message across a partner's reviews. They use the stored authored text and history when available. Jobs that need judgment, such as writing summaries or choosing facts to file, use the mailbox partner's Doc link first under decision 195. Decision 196 provides for prepaid Claude backup when the link is unavailable. Talking to Doc from a reply also needs [Doc in the app](https://github.com/DoctorCRE/doctorcre-engine/issues/955). See the [engine contracts](../new-database.md#home-inbox-reads-for-the-app).
