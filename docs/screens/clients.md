# Clients

- [ ] The list and drawer show each of the client's deals from Open on the Deals board. Each name opens that deal at `/deals?deal=<id>`.
- [ ] A client with no active deal has no deal link. The list shows recorded facts without an alarm badge for inactive clients or a list of blank fields. The list and drawer use Territory.
- [ ] The drawer uses the glossary’s How found labels, including Not recorded. It shows clean North American phone numbers as `(202) 555-0100` and preserves other numbers and extensions.
- [ ] Notes expand with More when there is more to read. The view omits file citations. Details opens the record reference.
- [ ] Recent activity starts with the newest few entries and expands with Show all. Each entry uses plain words without email addresses. A recorded deal opens from the entry, and a saved email can be read from the entry.
- [ ] Recent activity shows, once each, the work filed to the client's deals and to a person's current companies or a company's current people. A person's coworkers at those companies are left out (coordinator, 2026-11-24). Current means as of the partners' Chicago day. An entry filed elsewhere names where, such as "on deal Example Suite Lease".
- [ ] A person who is both a client and a lead has one Recent Activity history. Either role reference opens that history.
- [ ] On engine sign-in, Closing anniversaries opens from More. It shows every closed-won deal whose closing anniversary falls within the next 30 Chicago days, with the client, deal, years and date. Blank names show Unnamed client or Unnamed deal. It shows an empty state when there are no matches and offers retry on failure.
